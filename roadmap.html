Roadmap

We're building this in the open. Here's what's live, what's in progress, and what's coming next. Have a suggestion? Let us know.

Articles of Expansion
◦

Brand Partners

Opening limited compendium brand partnership spots for established manufacturers. Brand partner presence is restricted to device-specific compendium pages — never reviews. All brand partnerships are disclosed.

◦

Community Giveaways

Devices and accessories donated by manufacturers and trusted artisans for review will be given away to the community after reviews are published. More on how this works as we launch our next round.

In the Forge
⚙

Smarter Search

Search the compendium by materials and features, not just device names. Type "quartz" and find every vaporizer that uses quartz components; type "induction" and find every induction-heated device.

⚙

Compendium Expansion

We're continuously processing the community archive to add more device articles. Coverage is prioritized by community discussion volume — the most-discussed devices come first.

Deployed to the Realm
✓

Community Review Submissions

Anyone can submit their own review of a vape through the Reviews page. Approved reviews land in the new Community Reviews section, where each contributor gets their own tab on the device's review page — alongside, not instead of, our panel reviews.

✓

Community Edit Pipeline

A complete community-edit loop. Click Suggest an Edit on any compendium article and the body becomes editable in place — text and photos. Articles flag where community help is needed; submissions land in a moderation queue, and approved edits (including user-submitted device images) go live within minutes.

✓

Honorable Mentions

We've started highlighting valuable community members — Discord or wiki contributions — in our newsletter. Sign up for the newsletter to see who's getting spotlit.

✓

Reviews Archive Migration

The full Round Table Reviews archive has moved from Muddy River Workshop to its permanent home here at theroundtable.wiki, with a full panel index and individual review pages.

✓

Compendium Category Filters

Filter compendium articles by device type — Desktop, Portable (Battery), and Portable (Torch/Butane) — so you can browse by how you like to vape.

✓

Newsletter

The Round Table newsletter via Beehiiv — new reviews, compendium updates, and community news delivered to your inbox.

✓

Trusted Artisans Directory

A curated directory of cottage vendors, makers, and small-batch craftspeople vetted by the community.

✓

Round Table Reviews

Unsponsored panel reviews from the Highly Accessible Podcast crew. Five reviewers, one vaporizer, two weeks each — then we compare notes.

✓

The Compendium — Beta

Hundred-plus articles covering the most-discussed dry herb vaporizers, synthesized from fifteen years of archived community knowledge. Searchable, filterable by device type, and growing continuously.

Have a suggestion?

This is a community project. If there's something you'd like to see — a device we're missing, a feature that would help, or feedback on what's already here — drop it in the box. We read every one; good ideas land on the roadmap above.
